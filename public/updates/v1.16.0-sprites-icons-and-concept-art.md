title: Mediocre-sized update bringing new features!
---

- **Jot down an idea in seconds:** Press N (or Quick add) and type a name. Paste a whole list of names to make many at once.
- **Quick Add is in!** Each Fakémon has a status. Unfinished ones wait on a shelf at the top of My Collection, with a Continue button.
- **Notes and doodles.** Every Fakémon and every region has a private notepad, and you can draw in it. Only you see it.
- **Tags, a table view and a dex check.** Tag your Fakémon and search `#tag`. The new table shows your whole dex at once, and Region Analytics finds gaps in your Pokédex numbers.
- **Artwork sets.** An Artwork tab holds more official art, sprites, an icon and concept art, each with its own credit.
- **Right-click and Ctrl+K.** Right-click a card for everything you can do with it, and press Ctrl+K to search from anywhere.
