version: v1.14.1
date: October 11, 2026
title: Tips, polls and voting fixes
---

- **First-visit tips.** New pages show a few short tips the first time you open them. You can turn them off in Settings, or take them again from the start.
- **More choices for polls.** When you make a poll, you can hide the results until it closes, and choose who can see who voted: nobody, just you, or everyone. Voters can see your choice before they vote.
- **Ballots send again.** Submitting your ballot in an event could fail with an error saying 'column reference "x" is ambiguous'. That's now fixed.
- **A tidier voting form.** The score now sits next to each criterion's name, the stars are easier to tap, and the whole thing fits properly on a phone. The list of entries also keeps the one you're rating in view.
- **Pass on recommended moves.** In Recommended Moves, remove a move you don't want and the next one in line takes its place. "Show others" swaps a whole section, and you can bring back what you removed.
- **Badge and role notifications.** You now get a notification when you're given a badge or a role. You can turn the badge ones off in Settings.
- **Scheduled announcements.** Event organizers can write an announcement ahead of time and have it post when voting opens, a while before it closes, when it closes, or at a date and time they pick.
- **Some cleanup behind the scenes.** A few things in the database were out of step and are back in order.
