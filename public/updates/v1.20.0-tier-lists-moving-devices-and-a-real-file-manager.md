## New

- **Woobux!** Now we are truly one step closer to gambling inside Woogidex /j. Every account has a wallet, and it's all server-side, so nobody can just edit their balance (hopefully). Hit "Got it" at the bottom of this update for your first 500 Woobux (this is the first update that pays). Your balance is in your account menu and on the Fun tab. Soon you'll spend them on profile looks and the like.
- **The Fun tab.** The Battle tab is now Fun. Your Woobux, the daily streak, the shop (it opens soon) and the tier maker all live there, and Battle keeps a spot too while it takes a break. Collect today's Woobux right from the Fun home page and watch the coins fly into your balance. What I've noticed is that a lot of you drop by, make a Fakémon or two, and then I don't see you again for a while. That's totally fair, but I'd love Woogidex to be a place you want to open every day, not only when a new idea hits. So the Fun tab, Woobux and the daily streak are the first step of a long-term plan to give you more reasons to come back and hang out. I took a good amount of inspiration (for UI) from Chinese apps and sites here, since they're honestly the experts at making daily check-ins feel like these. More are coming, or not. Just tell me if you guys like it or not in feedback!
- **Daily login streak.** Come back every day for Woobux, and the deeper you are in the 30-day streak, the more you get. Days 7, 14, 21 and 30 also give you streak savers. Missed a day? Use streak savers to keep your streak alive. A new day starts at 12:00 am Philippine time.
- **Tier maker.** I got jealous of Tiermaker's update so I made my own. Add your Fakémon (or main-game Pokémon), every Fakémon in a save file, your own pictures, text tiles, or just words, then drag them into tiers, or tap one and pick its tier. Every item has its picture. Write why each one sits where it does, and save the whole board as an image. Post it on the Community Hub so others can rank their own copy, or send it in a chat with the trophy button. A Changes panel shows what moved since the last version, with little up and down arrows on each one.
- **Tier lists aren't just for Smogon tiers.** Name your tiers whatever you want, like "What they would do on a plane". Add columns, or turn any list into a pyramid, a numbered grid, an alignment chart or a quadrant chart.
- **Tier list game modes.** Blind ranking (one at a time, no take-backs), snake draft with your friends, a bracket, VS head-to-head, face-off, and keep, trade, cut.
- **More tier list stuff.** Private templates on your device, single-use lists that never save (for a quick picture), your own backgrounds and sizes, undo, and a presentation mode for streams. Adding stuff to someone else's list keeps it in your copy only.
- **Tier lists in groups.** In a group's Toolbox, make a list the group keeps, or one where everyone ranks and you see the group's average.
- **Roles for groups.** Owners can make their own roles (Tier leader, Event host, whatever you want) and pick what each can do: post, use the Toolbox, remove posts, let people in, ban, edit the group, run the chat. There's an "everyone" role too, for what every member can do.
- **Group chats for groups.** Group owners and mods can connect one of their group chats to the group (Manage > Group chat). Then members join it right from the group page with one tap. Leave the group, and you leave the chat too. Messages are still end-to-end encrypted.
- **Files in posts.** When we have something for you to grab, like a patch or a sprite pack, we'll post it on the Community Hub with a Download button.
- **A game picker.** Top left, next to the logo. It's Pokémon for now, but Temtem, Roco Kingdom and Palworld are on the list. This was promised to us 3000 years ago.
- **Your own Pokédex colours and egg groups.** Hit "+ Custom" on the colour swatches or in the egg group list to make your own.
- **Move modifiers.** You don't need blocks or code for most move effects anymore. In the move editor, Battle effect now has a Modifiers tab right next to Blocks and Code. Add an effect, type its chance, and pick what it does: a 30% freeze chance, 20% burn, stat drops, draining, recoil, high crit, switching out, and more. "Behaves like" makes a move work like a main-game one, like Pursuit.
- **Showdown export for tiers.** "Export as" gives you a `formats-data.ts` with every tier in it, so the devs of other Showdown servers can paste the changes straight in. You can also copy the tiers as plain text, or paste in a list like "OU:" then names to fill a board.
- **Move your stuff to another device.** Getting a new phone? Settings > Data & Storage > Move to another device. Press Send on the old one, type the code it shows on the new one, and your whole collection goes straight across. It goes from browser to browser, so nothing gets stored on our servers. Sign in to the same account on both.
- **My Collection works like a file manager now.** Self explanatory.
- **Your own universal moves.** Every region can have its own list of universal moves now, or use one generation's list, from Gen 1 to Gen 9. You can also change the global list everyone else uses. It's on the region's page, in Settings > Editor & Tools, and there's a Change list button right in Browse Moves.
- **Zoom and pan in the evolution editor.** Drag the empty space to move around, Ctrl + scroll (or pinch) to zoom, and the buttons in the corner zoom in, zoom out or fit everything in view. Huge evolution lines are finally not a pain to work with.
- **An anti-bot check on sign in.** Signing in, signing up and resetting your password now have Cloudflare's check (the little box that usually just says "Success!"). It keeps bots from guessing passwords.

## Changes

- **"Like Triple Axel" is now "Rising".** And you get to pick how the power grows i.e. linear (like Triple Axel), additive, or exponential.
- **The search bar is smaller and sits by the icons.** It shows Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) too, the shortcut for quick search.
- **Big regions load faster.** Clicking a region with tons of Fakémon, moves and so on used to freeze for a bit, because it drew everything at once. Now it only draws what you can see, and draws more as you scroll.
- **Conditions act like everything else.** They get the same right-click menu as moves and abilities i.e. cut, copy, paste, folders, drag into folders, sorting, and the Delete key.
- **Fakémon art uses one standard way to load everywhere.** Contest results, the Community Hub and profiles all load art the same way now, through the art shield, and they try again if the internet hiccups.
- **A security sweep.** I went through who can see and do what on the server. A couple of small things got tightened overall.
