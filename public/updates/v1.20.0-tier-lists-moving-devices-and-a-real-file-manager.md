## New

- **Woobux!** Our own currency. Every account has a wallet, and it all lives on our server, so nobody can just edit their balance. Hit "Got it" at the bottom of this update for your first 500 Woobux (this is the first update that pays). Your balance is in your account menu. Soon you'll spend them on profile looks and the like.
- **Daily login streak.** Come back every day for Woobux, and the deeper you are in the 30-day streak, the more you get. Days 7, 14, 21 and 30 also give you save passes. Missed a day? Use a save pass to keep your streak alive. A new day starts at 12:00 am Philippine time.
- **Badges are their own thing now.** Roles (Admin, Moderator and so on) are what let staff do stuff, and badges are just for looks. A role can give you a badge, and there are new badges to earn: Bug Hunter, Supporter, ones for publishing 1, 10, 50 and 100 Fakémon, and one for a 30-day login streak. Every badge got a new look too, a bit like Discord's.
- **The Toolbox.** A home for little apps, like Facebook's games and widgets. It's the toolbox icon on the top bar, or woogidex.com/toolbox. Every group has its own Toolbox tab too, no setup needed.
- **Tier maker, the first Toolbox app.** I got jealous of Tiermaker's update so I made my own. Add your Fakémon (or main-game Pokémon), every Fakémon in a save file, your own pictures, text tiles, or just words, then drag them into tiers, or tap one and pick its tier. Every item has its picture. Write why each one sits where it does, and save the whole board as an image. Post it on the Community Hub so others can rank their own copy, or send it in a chat with the trophy button. A Changes panel shows what moved since the last version, with little up and down arrows on each one.
- **Tier lists aren't just for Smogon tiers.** Name your tiers whatever you want, like "What they would do on a plane". Add columns, or turn any list into a pyramid, a numbered grid, an alignment chart, a quadrant chart or a Mount Rushmore.
- **Tier list game modes.** Blind ranking (one at a time, no take-backs), snake draft with your friends, a bracket, VS head-to-head, face-off, and keep, trade, cut.
- **Live tier lists.** Streaming? Go live and your viewers vote where each item goes, in real time, with a join code. In a live event, you show one item at a time and everyone votes on that one.
- **More tier list stuff.** Private templates on your device, single-use lists that never save (for a quick picture), your own backgrounds and sizes, undo, and a presentation mode for streams. Adding stuff to someone else's list keeps it in your copy only.
- **Tier lists in groups.** In a group's Toolbox, make a list the group keeps, or one where everyone ranks and you see the group's average.
- **Roles for groups.** Owners can make their own roles (Tier leader, Event host, whatever you want) and pick what each can do: post, use the Toolbox, remove posts, let people in, ban, edit the group, run the chat. There's an "everyone" role too, for what every member can do.
- **Group chats for groups.** Group owners and mods can connect one of their group chats to the group (Manage > Group chat). Then members join it right from the group page with one tap. Leave the group, and you leave the chat too. Messages are still end-to-end encrypted.
- **Downloads.** There's a Downloads page now (account menu > Downloads). It's where we'll put files for you to grab.
- **A game picker.** Top left, next to the logo. It's Pokémon for now, but Temtem, Roco Kingdom and Palworld are on the list. Yes, that's a hint.
- **Your own Pokédex colours and egg groups.** Hit "+ Custom" on the colour swatches or in the egg group list to make your own.
- **Move modifiers.** You don't need blocks or code for most move effects anymore. In the move editor, open Modifiers and pick them: a 30% freeze chance, 20% burn, stat drops, draining, recoil, high crit, switching out, and more. "Behaves like" makes a move work like a main-game one, like Pursuit.
- **Showdown export for tiers.** One button gives you a `formats-data.ts` with every tier in it, so the devs of other Showdown servers can paste the changes straight in. You can also copy the tiers as plain text, or paste in a list like "OU:" then names to fill a board.
- **Move your stuff to another device.** Getting a new phone? Settings > Data & Storage > Move to another device. Press Send on the old one, type the code it shows on the new one, and your whole collection goes straight across. It goes from browser to browser, so nothing gets stored on our servers. Sign in to the same account on both.
- **My Collection works like a file manager now.** Drag on an empty spot to draw a box and select a bunch of things at once. Ctrl + click adds one, Shift + click selects a range, Ctrl + A grabs everything. On phones, long press a card to start selecting, then tap the others. Then cut, copy, delete, take them out of a folder, or drag them all into a folder (or onto a region) in one go. It works in the table view too.
- **Your own universal moves.** Every region can have its own list of universal moves now, or use one generation's list, from Gen 1 to Gen 9. You can also change the global list everyone else uses. It's on the region's page, in Settings > Editor & Tools, and there's a Change list button right in Browse Moves.
- **Zoom and pan in the evolution editor.** Drag the empty space to move around, Ctrl + scroll (or pinch) to zoom, and the buttons in the corner zoom in, zoom out or fit everything in view. Huge evolution lines are finally not a pain to work with.
- **An anti-bot check on sign in.** Signing in, signing up and resetting your password now have Cloudflare's check (the little box that usually just says "Success!"). It keeps bots from guessing passwords.

## Changes

- **"Like Triple Axel" is now "Rising".** And you pick how the power grows: linear (like Triple Axel), additive, or exponential.
- **The profile preview looks like Discord's.** Hover someone's name and you get their banner, picture, badges, about me, when they joined and their role, instead of the old trainer card.
- **The tier maker has its own page,** with more room for everything.
- **The game picker is smaller,** and the coming soon window looks like the battle one.
- **Big regions load faster.** Clicking a region with tons of Fakémon, moves and so on used to freeze for a bit, because it drew everything at once. Now it only draws what you can see, and draws more as you scroll.
- **Conditions act like everything else.** They get the same right-click menu as moves and abilities: cut, copy, paste, folders, drag into folders, sorting, and the Delete key.
- **Fakémon art uses one standard way to load everywhere.** Contest results, the Community Hub and profiles all load art the same way now, through the art shield, and they try again if the internet hiccups.
- **A security sweep.** I went through who can see and do what on the server. A couple of small things got tightened overall.
