Hey, Woogi / Inoue here.

First, sorry if it feels like I keep changing stuff. Woogidex started as a tool just for me, and I still haven't gotten that out of my head. So I end up changing things on a whim, or just based on how I feel that day. The Woogidex account is one of those: I tried posting updates from it for a bit, and I didn't like it. So it's gone, and updates are back to this new window. I'm also trying out update notes that are more thought out and more personal, like this one. Peep the new update UI!

This one's big. Woogidex moved to its own address, I rebuilt how it works on phones, and the learnset editor got redone from scratch. There's also a pile of smaller stuff I kept putting off. Here's all of it.

## New

- **Woogidex lives at woogidex.com now.** The old address only helps you move your stuff. Open dex.woogi.xyz on a device you used before and it sends your collection, regions, custom moves and settings over to woogidex.com. Nothing gets overwritten and your old copy stays where it was. After that, sign in again on the new address.
- **Real links.** Every page has its own address now, so you can hover a link to see where it goes, copy it, or open it in a new tab. Refreshing keeps you on the same page, and Back and Forward finally work.
- **A new learnset editor.** The Moves tab has one search bar, like Showdown's teambuilder. It filters the moves you already have, and every other move that matches shows up greyed out under them. Click one to add it, or press Enter to add the top one. "Add as" picks how new moves are learned (none, Lv, TM or Egg).
- **The search speaks /ms.** Commas mean "and", `|` means "or", `!` means "not". Try `fire, physical, bp > 80`, `contact, priority > 0`, `raises atk, status`, `burn | paralyze`, or `"trap"` to search descriptions. The ? next to the box has the full list.
- **Table or grid.** The learnset is a real table with sortable columns (click a name to sort, again to flip it, a third time to go back). If you like cards better, the switch next to "Add as" turns it into a grid, and it remembers your pick on that device.
- **Recommend Moves got redone.** It used to show 3 or 4 moves a section and you had to roll the dice over and over to see more. Now every move that fits is in the list, the best picks first and the rest behind "Show more". Tick the ones you want and add them all at once. The x on a move stops it from showing up, either for just this Fakémon (saved with it) or for every Fakémon. "Kept out" at the bottom lists them if you change your mind. Generate Learnset skips them too.
- **Your own move flags.** Make up flags for your custom moves (like "Lunar" or "Cursed") with a name and a description. They show on the board, in exports, in cloud backup, and now in Browse Moves as filters too.
- **Quick commands.** Press `/` (or pick Quick commands in the right-click menu) and type a Showdown-style command: `/dt`, `/ms`, `/ds`, `/calc`, `/bulk`, `/weakness`, `/coverage`, `/learn` and `/randpoke`. Results use the real type pills with their icons and the category badges, so a list of moves or Pokémon is easy to read at a glance. On a phone, the + in the tab bar has Quick commands and Quick search, plus the other stuff a right-click gives you on a computer.
- **/calc reads like Showdown's calc.** You can give it EVs, natures, items and abilities now, like `/calc 252+ Atk Choice Band garchomp earthquake vs 252 HP / 4 Def eviolite toxapex`. It knows the items and abilities that change damage (Choice Band and Specs, Life Orb, Eviolite, Assault Vest, Huge Power, Multiscale, Levitate and a bunch more). Leave the EVs out and you get the old 252+ against no and full bulk. The result is a card now: both Pokémon with their types, the move, an HP bar for each spread, and whether it's an OHKO, 2HKO and so on. It's also a normal calculator, like on Showdown: `/calc (2 + 3) ^ 2 * pi` works, and so does `/calc 255 in hex`.
- **The art viewer.** Click the art on a Pokédex board to see it full screen. Zoom with the wheel, a pinch, a double-tap or the +/− buttons, and drag to look around. The arrows (or a swipe) go through every artwork set, and the sparkle shows the shiny.
- **A notifications page.** The bell has a "See all" page with tabs: All, Mentions, Likes, Follows and Updates. On a phone the bell opens it right away.
- **More notifications.** You hear about it when someone reacts to your Fakémon, posts or comments, and when an event you entered or follow shows its results (with your place, if you got one). A bunch of reactions on the same thing shows as one line, like "Ana and 4 others reacted to Emberpup". You can turn each kind on or off in Settings > Notifications.
- **@mentions in bios and art credits.** Type @ to tag someone in your bio or in your Fakémon's art credits. People in your credits only hear about it when you publish, not while it's sitting in your collection.
- **Switch accounts.** Keep up to 5 accounts on one device and switch with one tap from the account menu.
- **Hashtags.** #tags in posts are clickable. Search `#tag` to see every post with it, plus some related tags. Searching a plain word also gives you a "See posts tagged #word" shortcut.
- **Publish from the menu.** Right-click (or long-press) a Fakémon in My Collection and pick Publish to Community Hub.
- **Paste to import.** Import in My Collection has a Paste text tab now. Paste a plain text export or JSON, even a bunch of Fakémon at once.
- **Interface size.** Settings > Appearance has a slider to make everything bigger or smaller (80% to 130%).

## Changes

- **Phones feel like an app.** I redid the whole phone layout. There's a tab bar of icons along the bottom, menus and windows slide up from the bottom, the editor takes the whole screen, and you can swipe left or right to change tabs. You pick which tabs show in Settings > Appearance > Phone tab bar.
- **Phones fit more on the screen.** Things were way too big on phones before (my words were "granny phone"). Now a phone draws the site at about 80%, like a computer at Interface size 80%, so you get way more room. Pinch-zoom still works if something's too small, and the Interface size slider still works on top of it.
- **What's New and Updates are back.** The Woogidex account is gone, so update notes live in Updates in the account menu again, like they used to. After an update, a window like this one shows what changed. The pinned row in your notifications opens it again.
- **No more upload limit.** Publish as many Fakémon as you want. To keep spam out there's a speed limit instead: if you publish or post a lot in a short time, the site asks you to wait a few minutes. New accounts get a slightly smaller limit.
- **The Community Hub is open to everyone.** No account? You can still look around the Community tab and see open Fakémon. Sign up to see the rest :3 There's just one sign-up box at the top of Home now, not two.
- **Home and Browse.** "Feed" is Home now, and "Browse Fakémon" is just Browse.
- **The menu button is a menu icon when you're signed out.** It used to be an empty profile picture, which made no sense when there's no profile yet.
- **Settings explain themselves.** I rewrote the descriptions so each one says what it actually changes and when you'd want it, instead of just repeating the setting's name.
- **Nicer move categories.** Physical, Special and Status have new badges with their own icons. The Pokédex board and the Analysis charts use the same colors now, offline and in PNG exports too.
- **Publishing got cleaned up.** The publish and update-listing windows have search, sorting, cards with types and BST, and a clear "Selected" line. You only agree to the Community Rules once, then it's a quick confirm with the "Make it open" option.
- **Darker dark mode.** Dark mode is a bit darker with no blue tint, and divider lines are a tiny bit lighter so things are easier to tell apart.
- **iPhones no longer zoom in on text boxes.**

## Fixes

- **Interface size broke clicks and effects.** At anything other than 100%, right-click menus, tips, the emoji picker, dragging stuff around, drawing in notes, the art viewer, cropping and the paint on the front page all landed in the wrong spot. They follow your pointer at every size now.
- Posts on phones had no room on the sides and took up the whole screen. They sit in from the edges now, and pictures are shorter, so scrolling the feed is less tiring.
- "Keep reading" shows on all long posts and opens the rest right there.
- Double-clicking a collection card opens the preview without leaving the menu stuck open.
- The Analysis tab is as wide as the other tabs.
- The Draft/Done button next to the editor title is gone (you can still mark a Fakémon done from its menu).
- The messages button slides out of the way while the editor is open.
- The learnset's search box and columns line up properly on computers and phones.
- The Mega Evolution and Forme Change checkboxes aren't squished against their labels anymore.
