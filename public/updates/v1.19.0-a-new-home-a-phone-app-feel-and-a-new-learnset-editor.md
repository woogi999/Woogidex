---
version: v1.19.0
date: November 4, 2026
title: A new home, a phone-app feel and a new learnset editor
---

- **Woogidex has a new home: woogidex.com.** The old address only helps you move your stuff now. Open dex.woogi.xyz on a device you used before, and it sends your collection, regions, custom moves and settings over to woogidex.com. Nothing gets overwritten, and your old copy stays where it was. Then sign in again on the new address.
- **Real links everywhere.** Every page has its own address. Hover a link to see where it goes, copy it, or open it in a new tab, just like any other website. Refreshing keeps you on the same page, and Back and Forward work.
- **A new learnset editor.** The Moves tab has one search bar, like Showdown's teambuilder. It filters the moves you already have, and every other move that matches shows up greyed out underneath. Click one to add it, or press Enter to add the top one. "Add as" picks how new moves are learned (—, Lv, TM or Egg).
- **It speaks /ms.** The search works like Showdown's move search. Commas mean "and", `|` means "or", `!` means "not". Try `fire, physical, bp > 80`, `contact, priority > 0`, `raises atk, status`, `burn | paralyze`, or `"trap"` to search descriptions. Press the ? for the full list.
- **Table or grid.** The learnset is a real table with column names (Name, Type, Cat, BP, Acc, PP, Prio, Learned). Click a name to sort, again to flip it, and a third time to go back. Prefer cards? The switch next to "Add as" turns the learnset into a grid. Your pick sticks on this device.
- **Browse Moves is back.** The Browse Moves button opens the card browser with all its filters again, right next to the new search.
- **Sort every table.** The column names on every table sort it now: your collection, the Community Hub, the Moves tab and the rest.
- **Your own move flags.** Make up flags for your custom moves (like "Lunar" or "Cursed"), with a name and a description. The flag picker has search and sorting, and your flags show on the board and in exports. Cloud backup keeps them too.
- **Quick commands.** Press `/` (or pick Quick commands in the right-click menu) and type a Showdown-style command: `/dt` for data, `/ms` for move search, `/ds` for dex search, `/calc`, `/bulk`, `/weakness`, `/coverage`, `/learn` and `/randpoke`.
- **Nicer move categories.** Physical, Special and Status have new badges with their own icons. They match the type badges and look the same everywhere, offline and in PNG exports too.
- **The art viewer.** Click the art on any Pokédex board to see it on the whole screen. Zoom with the wheel, a pinch, a double-tap or the +/− buttons, and drag to look around. The arrows (or a swipe) go through every artwork set, and the sparkle shows the shiny.
- **Phones feel like an app now.** The whole phone layout got redone: smaller buttons and text with more space around them, and a tab bar of icons along the bottom. Pick which tabs it shows in Settings > Appearance > Phone tab bar. Menus and windows slide up from the bottom, the editor takes the whole screen, and you can swipe left or right to change tabs. iPhones no longer zoom in on text boxes.
- **Long-press menus.** On phones and tablets, hold your finger on a card to open its menu. It's the same menu as right-clicking.
- **A notifications page.** The bell has a "See all" page with tabs: All, Mentions, Likes, Follows and Updates. On a phone, the bell opens it right away.
- **More notifications.** You hear about it when someone reacts to your Fakémon, your posts or your comments, and when an event you entered or follow shows its results (with your place, if you got one). A bunch of reactions on the same thing shows as one line, like "Ana and 4 others reacted to Emberpup". Turn each kind on or off in Settings > Notifications.
- **@mentions in bios and art credits.** Tag people in your bio, and in the art credits of your Fakémon: type @ and pick them. People in your credits only hear about it when you publish the Fakémon, not while it's still in your collection.
- **What's New.** After an update, a window shows what changed (that's this!). Open it again from the pinned row in your notifications, or see every update ever from Updates in the account menu.
- **Updates are back in the menu.** The Woogidex account is gone. Update notes live in Updates again, like they used to.
- **No more upload limit.** Publish as many Fakémon as you want. To keep spam out, there's a speed limit instead: publish or post a lot in a short time and the site asks you to wait a few minutes. New accounts get a slightly smaller limit.
- **Switch accounts.** Keep up to 5 accounts on one device. Add one from the account menu, then switch with one tap.
- **The Community Hub is open to everyone.** No account? You can still look around the Community tab and see open Fakémon. Sign up to see the rest :3
- **Home and Browse.** "Feed" is now Home, and "Browse Fakémon" is now just Browse.
- **Hashtags!** #tags in posts are highlighted and clickable. Search `#tag` to see every post with it, newest first, plus some related tags. Searching a plain word also gives you a "See posts tagged #word" shortcut.
- **Publishing got a glow-up.** The publish and update-listing windows have a real layout: search, sorting, cards with types and BST, and a clear "Selected" line. You only agree to the Community Rules once. After that it's a quick confirm with the "Make it open" option.
- **Publish from the menu.** Right-click (or long-press) a Fakémon in My Collection and pick Publish to Community Hub.
- **Interface size.** Settings > Appearance has a slider to make everything bigger or smaller (80% to 130%). It starts at 100%.
- **Paste to import.** Import in My Collection has a Paste text tab. Paste a plain text export or JSON, even a bunch of Fakémon at once.
- **Darker dark mode.** Dark mode is a bit darker and has no blue tint any more. Divider lines are a tiny bit lighter, so things are easier to tell apart.
- **Fixes.** "Keep reading" shows on all long posts and opens the rest right there. Double-clicking a collection card opens the preview without leaving the menu stuck open. The Analysis tab is as wide as the other tabs. The Draft/Done button next to the editor title is gone (you can still mark a Fakémon done from its menu). The messages button slides out of the way while the editor is open. The learnset's search box and columns line up properly on computers and phones.
