title: Reports, appeals and comment controls
---

- **Reports.** The ⋯ menu on a post, comment, Fakémon or profile has a Report option. A report goes to the moderators with a reason and optional details. The person you report does not see your name.
- **Report status.** Settings > Safety lists the reports you sent and the result of each one. You get a notification when a moderator closes a report.
- **Hidden words.** Settings > Safety holds a list of up to 200 words or phrases. A comment on your post, Fakémon, wall or event that contains one of them is hidden from other people. The writer still sees it. You can unhide it from the same page.
- **Comment controls.** For each post, Fakémon and wall, you can set who can comment: everyone, followers only, or nobody. Slow mode sets a minimum time between two comments from one person.
- **Hide a comment.** The owner of a post, Fakémon, wall or event can hide one comment from other people.
- **Account history and appeals.** Settings > Safety lists the moderation actions on your account for the last year. You can appeal most actions within 30 days. A different moderator reviews each appeal, and you get a notification with the result.
- **Moderation tools.** Moderators get new review tools for spam and rule breaks. Some Community Hub actions can be unavailable on some networks.
