title: A safer Community Hub
---

- **Report button.** See something that breaks the rules? Open the ⋯ menu on a post, comment, Fakémon or profile and pick Report. Only the moderators see who sent it.
- **Follow your reports.** Settings > Safety shows what happened to each report you sent.
- **Hidden words.** Add words or phrases in Settings > Safety, and comments on your things that use them stay hidden from everyone else. You can unhide any of them.
- **Your comments, your rules.** Choose who can comment on your posts, Fakémon and wall: everyone, only followers, or nobody. Slow mode is there too. You can also hide a single comment on your own post.
- **Appeals.** Think a moderator got it wrong? Settings > Safety lists what happened on your account, and you can appeal it. A different moderator looks at each appeal.
- **Behind the scenes.** New tools help our moderators keep spam and trouble out of the Hub, so you can focus on making Fakémon.
