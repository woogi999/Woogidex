---
version: v1.18.0
date: October 26, 2026
title: Groups, business accounts and a new look
---

- **A new look.** The page, the header and the sidebar now share one color. Boxes around sections are gone: lines and space keep things apart. Corners are smaller, and nothing on the site blurs any more.
- **A new font.** Woogidex now uses BDO Grotesk everywhere. Code, exports and links keep a fixed-width font.
- **Groups.** Make a group for your art club, your region project or your battle team. A group is public (anyone can read and join) or private (a moderator lets each person in). Owners and moderators approve requests, remove people and remove posts. Each group has rules, an About page and a member list, and you can sort its posts by New or Top. Posts from your groups show in your feed.
- **One feed.** The For you, Following and Latest tabs are gone. The feed is one list, picked for you. "Featured this week" is gone too.
- **Messages in the corner.** On a computer, a round button at the bottom right opens your chats in a small window. It shows the faces of people who sent you new messages. The full-screen button opens the full Messages page.
- **Business accounts.** Staff can turn an account into a business account. It shows a category and an Email button, pins up to 3 posts to the top of the profile, and gets Insights about followers and posts.
- **News from Woogidex.** The Updates & Credits page is gone. News now comes as posts from the Woogidex account, which everyone follows (you can unfollow). Credits are in Settings > About.
- **Your uploads on your profile.** "My uploads" moved from the Community Hub to the Fakémon tab of your profile. Sort them by newest, oldest, name or most liked. Update and Unpublish are on each one.
- **Artwork scaling.** The Artwork tab in the editor has a Scaling option. Sharp (the default) keeps pixel art crisp. Smooth blends the pixels, which suits painted art.
- **Remove a background.** The scissors button on an image removes the plain background around it. It works on your device: nothing is uploaded.
- **Smaller fixes.** The Community Hub sidebar and the side column stay on screen when you scroll. Pages move in with shorter slides, and their parts arrive one after another. The Events address now stays in the address bar.
