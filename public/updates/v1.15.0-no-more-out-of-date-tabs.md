---
version: v1.15.0
date: October 19, 2026
title: No more out-of-date tabs, and a snappier site
---

- **Your work saves again, even with two tabs open.** Having Woogidex open in more than one tab used to throw up "This Tab Is Out Of Date" and quietly stop saving. Now the tabs just keep each other in sync, so you can edit in one and see it in the other. No reloading needed.
- **Way less lag.** Big collections were pretty heavy on phones and older computers, since every card was loading full-size artwork. Cards now use small previews, so scrolling should feel a lot smoother.
- **Templates for announcements.** Starting a "contest is open", "voting is open" or "here are the winners" post? Pick a template and just fill in the blanks.
- **No more flickery tickets.** The gold outline on event tickets no longer blinks on and off when your mouse is near the edge.
- **A friendlier place.** The chat filter catches more anti-LGBTQ slurs, including the sneaky spellings.
- **Fewer throwaway accounts.** If a lot of accounts get made from the same place in a short time, they'll need to confirm an email before they can post. Regular sign-ups aren't affected.
- **Cookie notice.** You'll see a small note about cookies the first time you visit. We only use them to keep you signed in, save your stuff and keep spammers out. No ads, no tracking. The privacy policy has the details.
- **Account deletion actually works.** If you asked for your account to be deleted, it now really gets deleted once the waiting period is over. It wasn't going through before, sorry about that!
- **A heads-up in the console.** If you ever open your browser's developer console on Woogidex, you'll see a big STOP sign. If anyone tells you to paste something in there, don't. It's a scam to get into your account.
- **Behind the scenes.** Better tools for our moderators, plus a bunch of security fixes to keep your account and info safe.
